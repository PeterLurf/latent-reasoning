# Prior check for §123: P-B continuous stabiliser (DITHER, FSQ+DITHER, optional contraction penalty) on §120's harness (2026-10-05)

Literature session, for Theory; Audit copied. Brief. IDs verified via abstract pages (2026-10-05) and LIT_INDEX.

## (a) Priors, and is it new?
- **Additive uniform noise as a quantisation proxy:** Ballé et al. 1611.01704 (a continuous proxy for the quantiser
  in end-to-end compression). **Agustsson & Theis 2006.09952** (universal quantisation: the uniform-noise channel is
  realised at *test* time with shared dither, removing the train/test mismatch).
- **Denoising recurrences:** 2510.14095 (error-correction corruption of latent states). Denoising autoencoders.
- **Contraction penalties:** Rifai et al. 1104.4153 (a Jacobian Frobenius penalty gives local space contraction; the
  precursor of contractive autoencoders). Stable Recurrent Models 1805.10369.
- **Attractor dynamics:** Attractor Models 2605.12466, DEQ 1909.01377.
  - **2609.26487 is the closest to your ρ measurement:** finished recursions are contractive along the motion
    direction but keep expanding directions (anisotropic stability), measured by perturbations.
- **Collapse dynamics:** 2609.02049.
- **Verdict: new as scoped.** Dither on a pretrained LM's Markov latent carrier, with ρ / basin as the mechanism
  measure and no-snap accuracy as the outcome, isn't found. The *intervention* framing (manipulate ρ, test whether
  no-snap follows) is what makes it informative.

## (b) Trap: ±0.5 dither on a step-1 grid is the quantisation proxy itself
- U(−0.5, 0.5) at unit grid spacing is exactly Ballé's training proxy for rounding. Transitions robust to it must keep
  states about one cell apart, so DITHER will likely learn **implicit quantisation** (discrete basins without
  explicit rounding).
- **That's a valid result,** "attractor states without explicit rounding", but it doesn't test a *continuous*
  stabiliser.
- **Recommendations:**
  - **Sweep the dither** (e.g. σ ∈ {0.1, 0.25, 0.5}) to get a dose response of ρ against no-snap. Small σ tests
    genuinely continuous contraction.
  - Measure **test-time clustering** of carried z_b (distinct clusters per true state). Clusters at grid points mean
    implicit quantisation.
  - For FSQ+DITHER, ±0.25 pre-rounding noise pushes code centres away from cell boundaries, so canonical codes are
    plausible. Optionally use universal-quantisation-style shared dither at test (2006.09952) as a variant.

## (c) The 8-dim bottleneck as a confound vs READ (768)
- **Yes.** The 8-dim tanh bottleneck is itself a capacity constraint that pushes towards discreteness, so DITHER-vs-READ
  differences mix the stabiliser with the bottleneck.
- **Add a within-harness control: an 8-dim bottleneck, continuous, no noise.** Compare DITHER, FSQ+DITHER and FSQ to
  it, not to READ.
- **Normalise ρ's perturbations to the state's scale** (per-dimension std of carried states). Otherwise ρ in a bounded
  8-dim space isn't comparable to ρ in 768 dims. Pre-register the perturbation magnitude.

## Notes on the claims
- **"Attractor strength is causal"** needs ρ to *change* and no-snap to follow, **across dither levels** (the dose
  response). A single on/off contrast isn't enough.
- **Codes-per-state and closure** for FSQ+DITHER: use Moore minimisation (QBN 1811.12530) to count states after
  merging behaviourally equivalent codes. "Closed" should mean the reachable set stops growing with length; report
  the curve.
