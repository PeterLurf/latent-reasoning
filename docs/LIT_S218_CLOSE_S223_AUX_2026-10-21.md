## §218: the facts being explained
- A1 (GPT-2 + an additive version bank, R2 recode targets, CurGate D 4 → 6 → 8, ProsQA-SYM, 2 candidates) fitted the template tokens (CE 0.24) and the recode targets (0.22).
- But it never fitted the 2-way candidate CE, even on **training** batches (≈ ln 2).
- CurGate never passed; both advances came on the 6k caps.

## Q1. Latent or memory state that learns its auxiliary targets but not the task [empirics]
- **Gatmiry 2609.19717 Table 1 (read), the closest.**
  - Task: shortcut-controlled reachability, where the distractor is the target's image in an isomorphic copy, so chance is 50.0. 2-layer GPT-2-architecture model from scratch.
  - At chance:
    - No-CoT 48.3;
    - **continuous thoughts at full budget from the start, no curriculum: 47.8**;
    - the curriculum without latents: 49.0.
  - Partly learns: written shortest-path CoT 63.5.
  - Solves it: Coconut 97.6 / 99.9 and ATC 99.9, **only with stage curricula gated on competence** (ATC on task accuracy ≥ 0.9; Coconut on intermediate accuracy).
  - **A1's CurGate advanced on caps without passing, so A1 is effectively the "no gated curriculum" arm.**
- **Coconut 2412.06769, Table 1** (GPT-2, no stage curriculum): GSM8K 14.4, ProntoQA 52.4 (binary, near chance), ProsQA 76.1 (≈ No-CoT's 76.7, the shortcut level). Appendix C.1 shows loss spikes at stage transitions.
- **Bowman 1511.06349 §3.1** (posterior collapse): the decoder "go[es] after low hanging fruit", a stable equilibrium in which the latent gets ~no gradient. Fixed by KL annealing and word dropout.
  - Analogue: pretrained GPT-2 fits the template and recode targets without routing the answer through the bank.
- **Ravichander 2005.00719:** a probe can read information the task does not use (decodable ≠ used).
- **ARMT 2407.04841** (delta-rule associative memory on GPT-2): needs a segment curriculum (2 → 32). Multi-hop QA3 falls 90.4 → 37.0 ± 10.3 as context grows.

## Q2. Optimisation pathologies [empirics unless tagged]
- **No partial credit on symmetric reachability:**
  - Mirtaheri 2505.21825 Thm 2 [theorem; heuristic query-model abstraction]: with too few queries, success is **exactly ½**.
  - Saparov 2412.04703: a plateau near loss 1; the probability of converging becomes "vanishingly small" as graphs grow; "increasing the scale … does not alleviate" it.
  - Together: the answer gradient is ~0 until the full reachable set is read.
- **Easy tokens absorb supervision:** Bachmann & Nagarajan 2403.06963 ("Clever Hans" on path-star; the decision token at chance); Frydenlund 2410.13779.
- **Auxiliary / main gradient conflict:**
  - Du 1812.02224 [theorem, small step size]: using the auxiliary gradient only when its cosine with the main gradient is ≥ 0 converges on the main loss.
  - PCGrad 2001.06782: the "tragic triad" of conflicting gradients, dominating gradients and high curvature.
- **Staged internalisation is fragile:** iCoT 2405.14838 (GPT-2): without removal smoothing or optimizer reset, accuracy drops to 0 and doesn't recover.

## Q3. Verdict on "an additive version-bank latent state that solves synthetic depth tasks did not learn a 2-candidate language graph task at GPT-2 scale": **NARROW**
- **Already reported:** chance on shortcut-free symmetric reachability without a competence-gated latent curriculum (Gatmiry 47.8 / 48.3 / 49.0; Mirtaheri's ½ theorem; Coconut-without-curriculum near chance on ProntoQA).
- **Also reported:** the task is **learnable** with gated curricula (Gatmiry; a small model from scratch). So no claim about what the bank or the scale *cannot* do.
- **Suggested wording:** *"Under our recipe (answer CE + per-pass recode targets, a depth curriculum that advanced on step caps without passing its gate), A1 did not leave chance on ProsQA-SYM's 2-candidate answer, even on training batches, while fitting its template and recode targets. This matches the chance-level arms of shortcut-controlled reachability without a competence-gated latent curriculum (Gatmiry 2609.19717: 47.8 / 49.0), which become solvable with gated curricula. A negative for this recipe, not for version-bank state on language."*
- **One difference worth stating:** Gatmiry's No-CoT **memorises** a fixed train set (train loss 0.009, val 0.44 → 1.40). A1's train-batch CE stays at ln 2. If A1 draws fresh items each batch, its training CE is effectively held-out, so this is not a contrast in fitting ability. Check the generator before citing it as a contrast. **Resolved 2026-10-21 (Theory, boxes/langvb.py:429):** A1 draws fresh items (prosqa_sym.Stream(22100), test / GATE signatures excluded, no fixed pool), so its train-batch CE is a held-out, pre-update CE. Contrast with Gatmiry on generalisation only, not on memorisation.
- **Most likely explanation the literature supports:**
  - The 2-way label has no partial credit (Mirtaheri, Saparov), so the answer gradient stays ~0.
  - Meanwhile the pretrained decoder settles in a Bowman-type equilibrium, fitting template and recode targets without using the bank (Ravichander: recode decodability ≠ use).
  - The ungated curriculum (caps) removes the one lever with positive evidence.
- **Diagnostic:** a frozen probe bank → answer above ½ would say the reachable set is present but unread. Under §222 PROBE-S, any such probe needs a content-equivariant positive control (Frame Lemma).

## §223 (PORT-STATE) addendum: does a per-latent auxiliary decoding loss block answer learning?
**(1) Evidence** [empirics]
- **On GSM8K-style latent CoT, per-latent decoding losses mostly HELP or are neutral. No report of them blocking the answer was found.**
  - **SIM-CoT 2509.20317:**
    - Loss: L = λ_step·Σ_k CE(decoder(z_k) → step_k) + λ_lm·CE(answer), with the gradient flowing into z (eq. 6–8, read).
    - It *fixes* latent collapse when K grows: "+8.2 % Coconut GPT-2; +3.0 % CODI LLaMA-3.1-8B" (abs).
    - On CODI-GPT2 it is roughly neutral: 42.6 vs 43.7 in MUX's Table 1; 42.08 vs 40.86 in PMPS's table.
  - **MUX 2607.18264:** a per-latent local KL lifts GPT-2 from 32.7 to 48.2 (Fig. 3). Its Table 4 (LLaMA-1B, local loss only, γ = 0): SIM-CoT-style decoding gives 31.6 vs MUX 48.7, so the **form** of the local target matters a lot.
  - **Cui 2602.22441:** strong per-step supervision (CoLaR) makes the answer depend on the latents (ProsQA → ~50 % at zero steps). That is the intended effect, not a block.
- **What separates §218 from these:**
  - a no-partial-credit binary answer;
  - an ungated curriculum;
  - the Clever-Hans token imbalance: §223's targets R_j (the whole remaining problem) are many tokens per latent against a short number answer.
- **I found no direct evidence on aux-target length vs answer learning.** Treat the imbalance as a risk for the $0 pilot to measure, not as a known effect.

**(2) Accepted remedies (listed, not recommended)**
- **λ warm-up / annealing:** Bowman's KL annealing analogue. Also the reverse, starting λ high and decaying.
- **Input dropout for the decoder** (Bowman's word dropout): weakens the shortcut path.
- **Cosine-gated auxiliary gradient:** Du 1812.02224 [theorem].
- **Gradient projection:** PCGrad 2001.06782.
- **Adaptive loss balancing:** GradNorm 1711.02257 (Chen et al., verified).
- **Per-token normalisation of the aux CE, or answer-token upweighting.** Addresses the token-count imbalance (Bachmann's "easy tokens" mechanism).
- **Stop-gradient on the aux decoder's input.** Caution: it turns the aux into a pure probe, and SIM-CoT / MUX deliberately let the gradient reach z.
- **Competence-gated curriculum:** Coconut / ATC gate on intermediate or task accuracy; an iCoT-style optimizer reset at stage changes.

## Addendum (§218 closed, Note 218-28, c6f9f5f6)
- DIRECT-SYM (plain one-pass GPT-2, same data and curriculum) is **also at chance** at every D (0.46–0.52, NO-ENTRY), with A1's signature.
- So the failure is **task × recipe**, not version-bank-specific. That matches Gatmiry 2609.19717's ungated arms: No-CoT 48.3, curriculum without latents 49.0, latents without curriculum 47.8.

- The NARROW verdict stands, with "task × recipe" as the scope.
