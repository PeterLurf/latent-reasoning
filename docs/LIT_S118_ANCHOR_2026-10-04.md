# Prior check for draft §118: no-snap latent chains on S5. ANCHOR (latent-path state ≈ stop-grad text-path state) vs LONG (k_train 8/16/32) (2026-10-04)

## (a) Priors
- **Matching free-running to teacher-forced dynamics: professor forcing 1610.09038.** It aligns an RNN's hidden
  dynamics when fed its own outputs to its dynamics when fed ground truth. **ANCHOR is the same idea:** latent-fed
  carried state ≈ text-fed carried state.
- **Aligning latent states to a text-path teacher:**
  - CODI 2502.21074 (answer-token hidden-state self-distillation from a CoT teacher);
  - KaVa 2510.02312 (KV trajectories);
  - ICoT-KD 2311.01460;
  - System-1.5 2505.18962;
  - LIFT 2609.38149 (teacher-forced state targets from a pretrained LM's distribution, so its own states are fed back
    at inference).
- **Drift and stability:**
  - fixed-point / attractor codes: DEQ 1909.01377, Attractor Models 2605.12466, 2609.26487 (anisotropic stability
    after completion);
  - norm-preserving channels: Howe 2609.18966, nGPT 2410.01131;
  - mixture-collapse dynamics: 2609.02049;
  - Coconut-family drift beyond trained budgets: our §13 / §64.
- **Covariate-shift fixes in sequential prediction:** DAgger 1011.0686 (train on your own states). ANCHOR instead
  forces your own states to *equal* the teacher's, which removes the shift. A complementary route.
- **New as scoped, not as concept:** professor forcing and CODI anticipate the alignment. New here: a
  per-hand-off anchor of a pretrained LM's carried latent to its own text-path code, aimed at no-snap stability beyond
  the trained horizon, scored by drift curves and donor SERIAL.

## (b) Collapse and trivial solutions
- **Stop-gradient self-distillation is known to avoid collapse only with care.** SimSiam 2011.10566 and BYOL
  2006.07733 need stop-gradient *plus* a predictor or asymmetry. Your asymmetry is the text vs latent input.
- Your fork/reader decode losses keep the latent informative, which blocks the constant solution.
- **Residual risks:**
  - (i) Norm shrinkage or growth in directions the decoders don't read. Relative MSE (÷‖target‖²) is scale-invariant,
    but it can still let the code drift along *unread* directions.
  - (ii) Rank collapse of the carried code.
- **Recommendation:**
  - cosine loss plus a log-norm-ratio term (|log‖a‖ − log‖b‖|), or MSE after per-coordinate standardisation (§73);
  - **monitor the effective rank** of carried latents over training (DeltaProduct's effective-rank measure; collapse
    shows as a falling rank) and the decode accuracy *from the latent path*.

## (c) LONG vs ANCHOR: the range principle
- **Yes, LONG most likely moves the boundary rather than removing it.** Our §41: trained depth sets reach at about 1.5–2×
  the training depth. Ouro degrades beyond its trained depth. A carried code never asked to be stationary keeps a
  generation- or age-dependent component (§56, §62).
- So LONG at k_train = 32 may pass at 512 if drift is slow, but expect failure beyond about 2× k_train.
- **ANCHOR targets stationarity directly** (THEORY §22's C2 homogeneity condition: no usable clock). The latent input
  distribution stays at the generation-1 code at every step. That is the principled fix.
- **But ANCHOR removes shift, not error.** Per-hand-off error still compounds (0.99 at 512 needs about 0.99998 per
  hand-off).
- Test both at 2,048 as well. A LONG pass at 512 that fails at 2,048 is the range-principle signature.

## (d) Wording
- **ANCHOR uses gold state text, so it is per-step supervised:** "C1 + C2 + C6 with per-step state supervision (not
  C4)". Same as READ.
- **"No snaps at test"** must mean that no text path, reader or decode feeds back at test. The anchor and the reader are
  training-only. State it.
- **A C4 version (anchor to self-decoded, final-answer-filtered text):**
  - the anchor then reinforces whatever code the model decodes, so wrong decodes become anchored attractors
    (confirmation bias; Arazo 1908.02983);
  - keep the final-answer filter in front, and report anchor-target precision against truth (logged only).
- **Avoid calling ANCHOR "fully latent / no supervision":** it is "fully latent at inference, text-anchored in
  training".
