# Prior check for §126: RAW-H vs ANNEAL (wide-then-narrow carrier) for the soft-phase no-read basin (2026-10-05)

Literature session, for Theory; Audit copied. Brief. IDs verified (abstract pages / LIT_INDEX).

## (1) Priors for annealed bottlenecks / "train wide, then narrow"
- **QBN 1811.12530 is the direct "wide then narrow" precedent for recurrent latents.** Train the continuous RNN first,
  then insert quantised bottleneck networks trained to reproduce its hidden states, then fine-tune.
  - The narrow code is **distilled from the wide state**, not left to learn on its own.
- **KL annealing** (Bowman 1511.06349): the KL weight ramps up from 0, so the latent starts effectively unbottlenecked
  and the bottleneck is imposed later. The same direction as ANNEAL.
- **Burgess et al. 1804.03599 (β-VAE) goes the other way:** it *increases* capacity over training (narrow → wide).
  Cite it as the contrast, not as support.
- **The warning from our own data:** ANNEAL's λ → 1 removes the wide path, exactly the §89 α → 1 / posterior-collapse
  pattern. If the decoder reads std(h) and W_up(z_b) isn't yet informative, accuracy collapses at λ ≈ 1.

## (2) Is RAW-H under FINAL a fair "no bottleneck" control? Yes, and it is the decisive one
- It isolates the bottleneck from the supervision. §111 differed in *both* (per-step targets plus a reader).
- Prior expectation from our data: §108 (raw latent, per-step decode targets, **no reader**) still hit the no-read
  basin in 2/4 seeds. So **RAW-H under FINAL may well plateau.**
  - If it does, the basin is a **supervision / reading problem** (FINAL and no reader), not bottleneck-specific.
  - The reader, or a label-free reading foothold (COPY, §124), is then the relevant fix, not ANNEAL.

## (3) Fix in advance
1. **ANNEAL's first 2k steps are RAW-H** (λ = 0 until 2k), and seed 2 plateaus by 2k. ANNEAL inherits whatever RAW-H
   does before the ramp. Either start the ramp only after a non-plateau check at 2k (label-free: loss below the ln-120
   null), or read both arms' plateau status at 2k as the same measurement.
2. **Make z_b informative before λ → 1 (QBN-style).** During the ramp, add a distillation term W_up(z_b) ≈ stop-grad
   std(h), so the narrow code is a compressed copy of the working carrier when the wide path disappears.
3. **Per-sample stochastic λ** (code dropout, ModDrop 1501.00102) instead of one global linear ramp. It avoids the cliff
   at λ = 1 (LIT_HANDOFF_COLLAPSE).
4. **std(h) statistics:** freeze them after warm-up (or re-estimate at the soft → hard FSQ switch; LIT_S125). Use the
   same frozen statistics at test.
5. **The stop rule's fallback** (restart-on-plateau at 2k) is legitimate as disclosed best-of-n with early triage
   (Dodge 2002.06305; LIT_STABLE_LATENT (b)). Report the restart count and compute.
