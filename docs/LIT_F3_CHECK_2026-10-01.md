# Pre-registration check: F3, compositional self-synthesis (THEORY §52 F3) (2026-10-01)

Literature session, for Theory; Audit copied. Extends `LIT_F1F5_CHECK_2026-09-30.md` §F3. IDs verified (arXiv API).

## 1. Composing solved easy problems into harder ones, with labels by construction
- **Rajaraman et al. 2606.27721:** theory of an autocurriculum that recursively decomposes long instances into short
  ones, solves them and *composes* the solutions. Supervision becomes subpolynomial in length (semiautomata). The closest
  conceptual precedent: F3 is a toy instantiation of it with a latent loop.
- **Lee et al. 2502.01612:** self-labels on n+1 instances with length and majority-vote filters. **No composition**: the
  labels are the model's direct answers on the harder instance.
- **MuSiQue 2108.00573:** builds multi-hop questions bottom-up by composing connected single-hop questions. A dataset
  construction, not self-training.
- **Skill composition:** Skill-Mix 2310.17567 (an evaluation); Zhao et al. 2409.19808 (fine-tuning on combinations of k
  skills generalises to unseen combinations). These compose *skills*, not solved instances.
- Also GECA 1904.09545, Absolute Zero 2505.03335, SCRL 2605.22074, Self-Improving Transformers (filters).
- **New if scoped as:** label-free depth extension of a latent loop using deep labels *composed* (AND over a known
  junction) from its own in-range answers. **Medium–low:** 2606.27721 is the theory, and MuSiQue/GECA are construction
  precedents.

## 2. Teacher-free deep supervision for looped models by composition
- Not found as such. Self-improvement (Lee) and the re-anchoring distillation ladder (our Pilot L/M) are the nearest

## 3. Label-error profiles across composition seams: a caution
- **Not studied in the literature found.** A simple calculation matters, though.
  - With in-range sub-answer error ε (≈ 0.15 from exp29) and roughly independent sub-answers, a composite label is
    correct only if *both* parts are right on positives. So its error is ≈ 2ε − ε² ≈ **0.28**, not "near in-range error".
  - Negatives are asymmetric: one confidently correct "unreachable" part suffices.
- **Consequence for §62's logic.** Composites (d7–12) would carry ≈ 0.28 error against ≈ 0.15 for single-episode labels
  (d1–6). That is **still a seam at 6/7**, smaller than T8's (0.38) but present.
- **Fixes, if F3 is meant to be "seam-free":**
  - (a) Also label shallow problems by composition (e.g. 3 + 3 for depth 6), so every depth carries a two-part error.
  - (b) The verification filter (keep only confident sub-answers), to bring composite error toward ε.
  - (c) Measure and report the label-error profile per depth before training, as exp29 did for T8.
- Pairing F3 with §62 this way makes F3 a second, independent manipulation of seam size (T8 0.38 vs composed ≈ 0.28 vs
  flat), rather than an assumed seam-free control.
