## A framing point on note 110-5 first
- The 4,105 transitions "never labelled by shorts" were **not unsupervised**. They occur inside long problems whose
  final answers (plus filtered self-training on the model's own chains) constrain them.
- So §110 s3 shows **transitions learned from long final answers, given a partial short foothold**. That is a C4-direction
  result.
- It is *not* the held-out-pair generalisation of grokking or §76(B): there, held-out entries never appear in any
  training signal.
- Word §112 accordingly: "how much short-problem coverage is needed for long final answers to supply the rest".

## §112: coverage dose-response
- **Priors:**
  - our **§85** (free table: certified 5/5 at p = 0.1, 2/5 at 0.03, 0/5 at 0.001: the closest dose-response);
  - §87 (shorts as a basin-entry device);
  - Wies 2204.02892 (sub-task supervision makes composites learnable);
  - Abbe 2306.16921 (mixed easy/hard curricula reduce sample complexity).
  - Held-out-pair priors, for contrast only: grokking 2201.02177 (a critical training fraction of a group's
    operation table); Chughtai 2302.03025; Lee 2606.07254 (held-out ordered generator pairs, S3×S3); §76(B).
- **Pitfalls in subsampling:**
  1. **Stratify** the transition subset by op and by state: each op keeps fraction f of its 120 start states, each
     state fraction f of its ops. Otherwise some ops get no shorts at all, and op-specific failure confounds coverage.
  2. A length-2 short uses two transitions, so require **both** to be in the subset.
  3. **Hold the short-problem count (the 5% budget) fixed across arms**, so only coverage varies, not dose.
  4. Fix the subset per seed and log it. Report never-labelled-transition accuracy **split by whether the transition
     occurs in the self-trained pool** (an indirect signal) or not.

## §113: a self-supervised reader (decodes the model's own decoded state text from [EOT, latent])
- **Priors:**
  - SELR 2608.13570 (the same model decodes its own latents back to steps);
  - ReLAT 2606.06252 (a question → latent → question reconstruction cycle);
  - 2606.20075 (generative reconstruction preserves latent–step information better than geometric imitation);
  - CODI 2502.21074 (self-distillation);
  - classic cycle consistency.
  - New as scoped: a reader trained only on the model's own final-answer-filtered decodes, used to make a carried
    latent readable from a fresh segment.
- **C4 holds:** no ground-truth intermediate labels. The targets are the model's own filtered decodes.
- **Risks:**
  1. **Self-referential collapse.** The decoder and reader can agree on a degenerate code (confirmation bias, Arazo
     1908.02983). Mitigated because the targets are final-answer-filtered (§110 precision about 0.98+). Keep the
     filter in front of the reader loss.
  2. **The reader's [EOT, latent] segment is position-0-safe** (the latent at position 1). Good; keep it.
  3. **It shows readability, not use.** Score C1 with §104's donor protocol as before.
