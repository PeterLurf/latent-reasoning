# Prior check for §130: an early label-free plateau marker (zb_std_in collapse) validated on a held-out card (2026-10-06)

Literature session, for Theory; Audit copied. Brief. IDs verified (arXiv API / abstract pages); Domhan is non-arXiv.

## (a) Priors
- **Early stopping of bad seeds or configurations:**
  - Dodge et al. 2002.06305 (bad fine-tuning seeds are identifiable early from validation loss);
  - Hyperband 1603.06560 and successive halving (budgeted early stopping on partial curves);
  - learning-curve extrapolation (Domhan, Springenberg & Hutter, IJCAI 2015, non-arXiv).
  - All use *loss or metric curves*, not an internal representation statistic.
- **Representation-collapse diagnostics:**
  - **active units** (Burda, Grosse & Salakhutdinov, IWAE 1509.00519: a latent unit is active if its posterior mean
    varies across inputs above a threshold). **zb_std_in is exactly an active-units statistic** for a deterministic
    code; cite it.
  - Representation-rank / std monitoring in self-supervised learning (VICReg 2105.04906 uses per-dimension std as the
    collapse signal).
  - Our own P7 (an in-training extrapolation predictor) and 123-3's flag.
- **Verdict:** using an active-units-style code-variance collapse as an *early, label-free predictor of a no-read
  basin*, validated prospectively on held-out seeds, is **new as scoped**.

## (b) Cross-card held-out: stronger, but ambiguous on failure
- Agreed that it is the stronger test of a task-agnostic marker. But if TEST fails, you can't tell "the marker doesn't
  generalise across seeds" from "it doesn't transfer across GPU classes" (bf16 numerics shift the dynamics; 125-2).
- **Cheap fix ($0):** add a **same-card held-out set** (4060, seeds 19–26) next to the cross-card one.
  - Pass both → robust marker.
  - Pass same-card only → card-specific thresholds.
  - Pre-register which one is primary. Cross-card as primary is fine.

## (c) n and the 0.9 bar
- With about 4 flagged seeds, precision and recall ≥ 0.9 means **zero errors** among positives. A marker with true
  recall 0.8 passes 4/4 with probability 0.8⁴ ≈ 0.41, so the bar is weak evidence.
- Even 10/10 gives an exact 95% lower bound of about 0.69 (Clopper–Pearson).
- **Recommendations:**
  - Since replays cost $0, **extend TEST until ≥ 8 flagged and ≥ 8 non-flagged seeds**.
  - Report precision and recall with exact CIs.
  - Keep "0 errors at n ≥ 8 per class" as the pass, i.e. a 95% lower bound of about 0.69 on each.
- **FIT overfitting:** 24 (θ × window) combinations selected on about 4 positives can overfit. Freezing before TEST (as
  planned) is the right guard. Also report the FIT-optimal cell's neighbours, since a flat optimum is more trustworthy.
