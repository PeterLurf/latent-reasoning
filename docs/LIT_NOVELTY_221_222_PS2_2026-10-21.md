## Suggested sentences
- **§221 / 221-2:** "On natural GSM8K, CODI-GPT2's latents (vectors or latent-position KV rows) do not port a donor's final answer (0.03 vs floor 0.008; cf. 2.5 % for whole latent-sequence transplants in 2606.01243). They do carry the donor's intermediate into the recipient's remaining computation (INT-FOLLOW 0.33–0.54 vs ≤ 0.04), reproducing SCIT's synthetic oracle-counterfactual pattern on natural pairs. The answer phase re-reads the question (QMASK → ≈ 0). Intermediates are decodable at alternate latents, as CODI §5.1 reported; we quantify this over all items against a floor."
- **PORT-STATE** (from the subagent, edited): "Unlike step-text (SIM-CoT, SELR, PLaT) or state-value (Thinking States 2602.08332) latent supervision, we train each latent so a question-blind decoder recovers the re-rooted remaining problem (the latent analogue of explicit rewriting: He 2512.02677, NRS 2402.17407; our synthetic §175). We test whether the latent prefix carries the task by swapping the whole donor prefix 1..t with the recipient's question visible, extending SCIT's step-t cache patching and Liang & Pan's position-swept state swaps."
