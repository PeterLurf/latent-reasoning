# QB0 (Note 263-5): question-blind sufficiency of the training law, and retargeting as the fix

Literature, written 2026-11-04 03:21 EST, at Theory's low-priority request (7e889ada). Our own bibliography was grepped first: Jiang & Bansal 1906.07132 and Kaushik 1909.12434 were already cited (LIT_PROSQA_SHORTCUT_2026-09-30, LIT_FREE_PAIRS_2026-10-08). There was no web sweep. All IDs below were checked against the arXiv API on 2026-11-04, and their claims are taken from the abstracts.

**One line:** the phenomenon is well known (**NARROW**). Question-only and passage-only sufficiency has been measured on synthetic generators (bAbI) and on multi-hop QA since 2018-2019. Retargeting is a known family of fixes (counterfactual / contrastive augmentation). What Note 263-5 adds is a generator-level *exact* audit: one explicit blind policy, enumerated over the training law, with a 1.000 sufficiency bound per depth. Prior work measures with trained blind models. Present it as a design guard, not a contribution.

|---|---|---|
| Kaushik & Lipton 2018, 1808.04926 (empirics) | Question-only and passage-only baselines do surprisingly well; passage-only models exceed 50% on 14 of 20 **bAbI** tasks, sometimes matching the full model; on CBT only the last sentence is needed | **Closest prior**: question-blind sufficiency in a *synthetic, templated generator*. QB0 is the same diagnosis made exactly (enumeration, not a trained baseline) |
| Chen & Durrett 2019, 1904.12106 (empirics) | Sentence-factored models that cannot do multi-hop still solve much of WikiHop / HotpotQA; spurious correlations make WikiHop solvable from the question and answers alone | Multi-hop data solvable without the intended input. The same family of finding |
| Min et al. 2019, 1906.02900; Jiang & Bansal 2019, 1906.07132 (empirics) | Single-hop shortcuts solve much of HotpotQA; adversarial distractor documents as a fix | The "single-hop shortcuts in HotpotQA" line Theory had in mind |
| Trivedi et al. 2020, DiRe, 2005.00789 (empirics + a formal probe) | A model-agnostic probe for *disconnected reasoning*, plus a **contrastive support sufficiency** transformation of the dataset that reduces it | The closest prior for a **generator-level audit + data-transformation fix**. Retargeting fills the same role: make the answer depend on the input that the shortcut ignores |
| Kaushik et al. 2019, 1909.12434; Gardner et al. 2020, contrast sets, 2004.02709 (empirics) | Minimal label-changing edits (counterfactual augmentation; local contrast sets) | Question-retargeting = a counterfactual edit of the *question* over a fixed context, changing the answer. Cite it as an instance of this family; the name "retargeting" is ours |
| Trivedi et al. 2022, MuSiQue, 2108.00573 (empirics) | Multi-hop questions built by composing single-hop questions to block shortcuts | A construction-time fix (an alternative to augmentation) |
| Zhang et al. 2022, *Paradox of Learning to Reason from Data*, 2205.11502 (empirics + a construction argument) | BERT learns statistical features inherent in a synthetic logic generator, and removing all of them jointly is infeasible | **Caution for the fix:** retargeting removes the LP feature, but other blind features (recency, which Note 263-5 leaves untested) may remain. Re-run the QB0 battery on the retargeted law, including a recency policy |

**Wording:** "On our training law, a question-blind policy is exactly sufficient at d 2-4 (cf. passage-only sufficiency on bAbI, Kaushik & Lipton 1808.04926; disconnected reasoning, Trivedi et al. 2005.00789). We add question-retargeting, a counterfactual question edit (Kaushik et al. 1909.12434; contrast sets 2004.02709), and re-audit the retargeted law." Make no "first" claim on either the audit or the fix.
