## 3. Prior for per-step latent + text decode on multiplication; length generalisation
- **Latent with a per-step decode:** none on multiplication. SIM-CoT, SELR and CODI are GSM8K only.
- **Most relevant: "Why Can't Transformers Learn Multiplication?" 2510.00184** (reverse-engineers ICoT
  multiplication models).
  - Standard fine-tuning hits a local optimum lacking the long-range dependencies.
  - An **auxiliary loss predicting the running sum** (a linear probe target) lets the model learn multi-digit
    multiplication.
  - That is a per-step state target, the same idea as your per-op side decode on "( S_j )".
  - Cite it as the closest prior for "running-sum supervision makes multiplication learnable".
- **Length generalisation:**
  - Implicit / latent methods: **none reported**. ICoT-SI trains and tests per size; 11×11 is only "partially
    internalized" at 0.74.
  - Explicit-format baselines: Cho 2410.15787 (position coupling + scratchpad, 10×10 → 20×15, ≥ 78.55% median) and
    Lee 2502.01612 (self-improvement, 5×5 → 9×9/10×10) (LIT_SOTA_TARGETS).
  - So "train 4×4, test 4×8 multiplier" with a latent method would be new.

## 4. Pitfalls
1. **The checkpoint leaves its range as j grows.**
   - In LSB-first format, step j's text "+ P_j ( S_j )" has P_j with **j leading zeros** (a shift counter) and S_j with
     about n + j digits.
   - At multiplier lengths beyond training, both the zero-count and the running-sum length exceed anything seen: the
     range principle.
   - **Fix to consider:** a **bounded checkpoint.** Once the low digits of S_j are final (they can't change after step
     j), emit them to the answer and drop them from the state. Keep only the unfinished high part plus the carry, about
     n + 1 digits.
   - Express the shift implicitly by position (as Cho's position coupling does), not with written zeros. Then the
     checkpoint size and content stay in range for any multiplier length.
2. **LSB-first tokenisation.**
   - GPT-2 BPE splits " 7 6 9 2" into single-digit tokens (spaces keep each digit separate), so the format is fine.
   - Pretrained numeracy is MSB-first, so expect little transfer from pretraining. That's acceptable, but don't
     attribute success to it.
   - Check that every digit and "+", "(", ")" is a single token.
3. **The operand stays visible, so watch for bypass.**
   - With a visible in every chunk and b_j as the op, the fork decode could recompute P_j directly from a and b_j
     (in-pass, about 1 step).
   - The running sum is the only carried state. GEN-M-MASK (latent-only fork) is the right control. Also report decode
     accuracy of S_j with slots ablated.
4. **Matched FLOPs.** ICoT-SI generates no CoT tokens at inference, while GEN-M runs latent slots plus snaps. Report
   FLOPs and sequential passes for all arms, as you plan.
