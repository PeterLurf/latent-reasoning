## 2. Mechanism

### 2.1 SLOT-LOCAL VERSION ATTENTION (SLVA), corrected

At pass t ≥ 1, for slot j and head h (12 heads × 64-d slices of N(V)):

U_t[j]_h = Σ_{s<t} α^h_{j,s} · N(V_s)[j]_h

α^h_{j,s} = softmax_s( ⟨W^h_q LN_q(N(V_{t−1})[j]), W^h_k LN_k(V_s[j])⟩/8 + β^h_0·1[s = 0] + γ^h·(t−1−s)·1[s ≥ 1] − c(step)·1[s ≠ t−1] )

- **Queries** are content queries from the slot's newest version. **Keys** are version s *at the same slot*. There is no position query and no wpe input.
- **The age term acts on s ≥ 1 only.** On V_0 it would be an absolute pass clock: V_0's logit relative to the newest grows as γ(t−1) (PROOFS row 8).
- **Own-slot keys are never pad-masked.** A padded slot then reads garbage, but finite garbage, and is masked downstream as before. Pad-masking them gives all −inf keys, which makes NaN reach real slots (PROOFS row 1).
- **Initialisation:**
  - W_q, W_k and LN_q / LN_k come from block 6 (as N2's gather);
  - γ^h = −(the ALiBi slope of head h);
  - β^h_0 = 0.
- **Distinct parameter names** (`slva_wq`, `slva_wk`, `slva_ln_q`, `slva_ln_k`, `slva_beta0`, `slva_gamma`), mode string "slva", and `args["arch"]` recorded (gate G-D0).
- **c(step) = c_at(step, 1000, 14000):** 1e4 until step 1k (exact CARRY), then 20 → 0 linearly at 14k. It is driven by a capability flag, not by `mode == "pa"` (gate G-D4).
- **N** is the per-slot RMS reset (pin 5).
- **Unchanged from N2:**
  - the prelude (blocks 0–5, V_0);
  - the core (6–9) and coda (10–11) with their bank reads over [all versions × all slots | current, causal], the β / γ bank biases and the per-head calibration at step 0;
  - R; R1, reweighted R2, and the R4 curriculum.
- **The pass-global bank is untouched.**

### 2.2 The position table: two arms

- **D3-T:** the table trains as in N1 / N2 (lr 1e-4 cosine, wd 0.01).
  - SLVA has no content-free query, so Prop 3′'s direct row incentive is absent [DERIVED, §3].
  - The expectation is an N1-like table: low decorrelation, and length-tolerant.
- **D3-F:** FROZEN-WPE.
  - The pretrained table, with `wpe.requires_grad = False` set **before the optimizer is built**. Otherwise decoupled decay still shrinks it by 2.38% over 48k.
  - The bytes are checksummed against the pretrained table at every checkpoint (G-D3).
  - Its support is per-step only: N1's per-step gain, and PA's binding (M3).
  - The swap evidence on rollouts points the other way. That evidence comes from a model trained on another table, so it predicts nothing for a model trained on this one [ASSUMED].
- **The two cells differ only in `wpe.requires_grad`.**

### 2.3 Second limit (trajectory drift): no rule-based fix

- **COPY-ANCHOR is excluded** from SLVA cells. Anti-drift must be *earned* by SLVA's own-slot reads, which is what N2's history does (M2), or by the core's V_0 reads. It is read causally (SELF-ABLATE, §5).
- **One optional add-on, chosen by the pre-stated $0 selector S0-1 on N1:** VOID key masks. Consumed (VOID) slots' current and stored keys are masked at every bank reader from the pass they become VOID. SLVA, which is slot-local, is unaffected.
  - This is §212 G1's compaction-as-key-masks without the restamp, with G1's registered fp64 gate.
  - If added, it goes into **both** cells.

### 2.4 Not included, each with its trigger

- **COPY-ANCHOR:** never in an SLVA cell.
- **Position randomisation / offsets in training** (sink kept): if a D3 cell reads ¬LENGTH-FREE with an absolute-position error locus.
- **CORE-BAND / EXIST-SCALE:** if a D3 cell reads LENGTH-FREE but fails STEP-GENERAL with errors at the handle. The ε trigger is re-derived from that cell's own late-slot error, not from N1's: PROOFS row 15 shows N1 itself would fire a 0.2% trigger.
- **REL-GATHER:** if LENGTH-FREE ∧ HISTORY-IGNORED and cross-slot gather use is wanted.
- **LONG-K training** (new; needs its own derivation before use).
  - *Trigger:* a cell reads LENGTH-FREE ∧ HISTORY-IGNORED ∧ ¬DEPTH-GENERAL.
  - *What it does:* some batches train the readiness-window answer loss out to K′ 24. Stability over many passes then becomes in-distribution, and anti-drift reads gain a training gradient.
- **Compaction + restamp:** if S0-1's descriptive gap share (GAPPED, §6) is ≥ 0.5.
- **TRUNC-LONG:** not needed (N1 is length-tolerant without long inputs).

### 2.5 Constraint check (pass-global attention memory)

- Every version stays stored and readable, and nothing is overwritten by rule (the anchor is gone).
- The pass input attends over all of the slot's versions. Every core and coda layer attends over all slots of all versions.
- CARRY is the degenerate pattern (one-hot on the newest). It is **not** credited as attention-memory use: the claim needs D3-C2-ATTN.

## 3. Mathematics (corrected statements)

**Theorem 1 (SLVA).**
- **(a) [PROVED]** At t = 1 the read has one finite-logit entry per slot and head, so α = 1 and U_1 = N(V_0) for every c and every parameter value. Pass 1 of an SLVA model is the CARRY architecture applied to its own weights; the mock is bitwise in fp32 and fp64.
  - **N1's length tolerance is a property of N1's weights**, not of the architecture.
- **(b) [PROVED UNDER** a logit spread < c − 104 (fp32) or < c − 745 (fp64), finite stored versions, the SLVA path with autocast off and no TF32**]** For c ≥ 1e4 the rollout equals CARRY bitwise (induction over passes), and SLVA's gradient is exactly 0.
  - **The K = 1, bank-masked model versus GPT-2:** exact in real arithmetic and ≤ 1e-9 in fp64 (gate_fp). Measured 5.4e-8 relative in fp32; SLVA adds zero error.
- **(c) [PROVED]** Slot-locality: U_t[j] depends only on (V_0[j], …, V_{t−1}[j]).
  - **Slot-local is not position-free.** Those vectors carry derived position (R² 0.9986), so α can be position-keyed, including at j ≥ 33 [OPEN]. That route is read by LENGTH-FREE-K.
- **(d) [PROVED]** ‖U_t[j]‖ ≤ √min(H, t) · max_s ‖N(V_s)[j]‖. This does not depend on T.
- **(e) [PROVED, per head]** Dilution acts only in t: ε = (t − |S*|)e^{−Δ}, and the deviation is ≤ 2Bε/(1 + ε).
- **(f) [PROVED from (e)]** Near-CARRY: α^h_{j,t−1} ≥ 1 − η ⇒ ‖U_t[j]_h − N(V_{t−1})[j]_h‖ ≤ 2ηB.

**Proposition 2 (decay-only rows) [PROVED + VERIFIED bitwise].**
- A row with zero gradient keeps m = v = 0 and changes only by p·∏(1 − η_tλ). The fp32 factor is 0.976177, the measured value.
- **D3-F has no table seam:** every row is pretrained.
- **D3-T** has an N1-like seam. N1 tolerates it.
- The **functional seam** (blocks trained only on positions < 33) remains in both [OPEN].

**Proposition 3′ (content-free gathers need row separation) [PROVED].**
- If adjacent content-free queries pick different keys with margin Δ, then σ_max(W_q^h)·‖p_j − p_{j+1}‖ ≥ 16Δ / ‖k_a − k_b‖.
- So either every row's gain grows, or the trained rows separate. N2's separation is measured to be row-borne: query cos 0.80–0.84 in range against 0.999 beyond.
- **SLVA's query is the LN'd content of the slot's newest version**, so no term in its loss requires raw-row separation [DERIVED].
- The residual route is a position-keyed α through derived position: Thm 1(c) [OPEN].

**Proposition 6 (why SLVA may collapse to CARRY) [DERIVED, first order].**
- ∂L/∂ℓ_s = α_s·⟨∂L/∂U, N(V_s) − U⟩.
- **On a copy slot,** stored versions differ only by the drift δ, so the gradient is O(δ). In range, N1's drift is small (acc 1.000 at n ≤ 8), so the gradient is near 0.
- **On a changed slot,** mixing the old symbol into the new value raises the loss, so the gradient points to the newest version.
- So in-range training gives SLVA little reason to read old versions. N2 had a reason SLVA lacks: its newest input was noisy (neighbour-mixed), so redundant own-slot versions paid.
- **Prediction:** P(HISTORY-USED) ≈ 0.15–0.3 per cell [ASSUMED]. If DEPTH-GENERAL holds without HISTORY-USED, the stability came from elsewhere, for example the core's V_0 reads or the table.

**Theorem 4 (the fresh step).**
- **(a) [PROVED UNDER A5′, COV′, P-FREE, hard aggregation]** Fresh-state COV′ is 1.000 at n 9–13 and 0.993–0.997 at n 14–16.
- **(b) [PROVED UNDER first-order composition]** At pass 1 a head sees ≤ 2T keys, so T* halves in the worst case.
- **(c) [PROVED] bar, [DERIVED] prediction.**
  - STEP-GENERAL ≥ 0.95 at T 55 needs a late-slot error ≤ 0.233% over the 22 slots beyond 33. It needs ≤ 0.12% if N1's in-range errors remain.
  - With region rates, N1 predicts **0.84–0.88**; measured 0.80 (m 16, T 55; 134 items).
  - The "error" is any per-slot error. At T ≥ 38 it is mostly spurious VOIDs, not copies.

## 6. Experiments

### Stage 0 ($0, local fp32, eval-only; pre-stated in note 212-15; Audit OKs $0 diagnostics)

**S0-1 SELECTOR on N1** (2070). Arms, on the RS items (exact decode at t = n − r):
- intact;
- restart (part_rs, compact);
- **MASKVOID:** the intact rollout with VOID slots' keys masked at every bank reader from the pass they become VOID;
- **REFRESH:** at every pass, slots whose decode equals their input class have their stored entry and their carry set to V_0[j];
- **GAPPED:** a fresh run from the exact decode, with the remaining symbols at their original positions and the consumed positions pad-masked.

Rule:
- D_R = pooled RS (restart − intact) over n 9–12, r ∈ {4, 6}, cells ≥ 100 items.
- G_MV = pooled over the same cells of (MASKVOID − intact).
- **ADD-VOIDMASK iff** G_MV ≥ 0.5·D_R **and** MASKVOID's control holds (acc_n2 at n 1..8 within −0.02 of intact). Otherwise no add-on.
- REFRESH's and GAPPED's shares are descriptive (GAPPED triggers compaction + restamp, §2.4). If interior pad masks are unsupported, GAPPED is omitted.

**S0-2 LENGTH-FREE-K reference** on N1 (2070), N2 (4060) and N3 (3070). This checks that the reading can fail before any cell relies on it.
- **Pre-stated:** N1 meets (a′); N2 and N3 do not.
- If N1 fails or N2 meets, (a′) is replaced before any D3 use, and no D3 rule cites it.

**S0-3 STAB-HIST on N2** (4060). It tests the thesis's mechanism.

## 7. Risks and falsifiers

1. **CARRY collapse** (P ≈ 0.7–0.85, Prop 6). The cell is then N1 with SLVA in form only. It says nothing against length, and it ends the attention-memory claim for this design. The follow-up is LONG-K (triggered, with its own derivation).
2. **Position-keyed α re-creates length keying at t ≥ 2.** *Falsifier:* LENGTH-FREE-K fails while NO-KNEE holds.
3. **The frozen table hurts the rollout,** as the swap did for N1. *Descriptive:* D3-F's DEPTH is below D3-T's while both are LENGTH-FREE (one seed).
4. **NO-ENTRY under the frozen table, or under SLVA.** *Falsifier at this budget:* no entry by 48k while N1 / N2 entered at 11k / 16k.
5. **Count decay at m 12–16** (STEP-GENERAL fails with LENGTH-FREE met). That triggers CORE-BAND, re-derived from the cell's own error.
6. **Seed lottery** (§199). One seed per cell, so a null is weak.

**Premise falsifier.** D3-T reads ¬NO-KNEE: training from pretrained without the content-free gather still produces a pass-1 cliff. That contradicts M1's per-slot attribution, and the next step is structural or relative positions in the core (v0 §1.5, row 2).

**Unreconciled, and moot for v1:** the anchored-value rate (PROOFS 0.14–0.57% against REDTEAM 2.8–10.4%). The two likely differ in which slot holds the value. COPY-ANCHOR is out of v1.
