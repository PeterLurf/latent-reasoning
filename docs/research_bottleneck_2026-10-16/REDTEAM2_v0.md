## Top 10 findings (ranked by likelihood × damage)

### 1. BLOCKER: R1-C makes the logged answer CE self-referential, and the curriculum gate and censoring read it
**Scenario.**
- Under R1-C, the answer target at t ∈ [n_i, K_i] is dec V_t's value whenever the decoded skeleton is "done" (S §2.1). The coda is then trained to copy whatever the version holds, right or wrong.
- vb_loss logs that loss as `answer_ce` [VERIFIED vbank.py:405–406].
- CurGate advances on it [VERIFIED vbank.py:558, 563 → 420–437].
- §211's NO-ENTRY censoring (answer-CE 1k-bins ≤ 0.5) and basin-entry dating read the same field.
- A model that reduces wrongly but self-consistently therefore advances the curriculum, reads "entered", and escapes rule b.
- It also blinds the one watch signal that caught N3: the answer_ce vs rec_ce divergence (211-12).
- This re-breaks REDTEAM F9b, whose fix was making the gate read answer CE instead of `tot`.

**Fix (pre-register; fixture-test in the scorer):**
- Log R1-C's loss as `answer_ce_self`.
- Compute a true-y answer CE every step (no gradient, same rows) as `answer_ce`.
- CurGate, NO-ENTRY, entry steps and the watch rules read `answer_ce`.
- Also log `commute_fb` and the true-target rec CE beside `rec_ce`.

### 2. BLOCKER (constraint): VB-2 re-adopts three things the registered design rejected on the user's constraint
- **CARRY as the base input.** REDTEAM F4: "U = N(V_{t−1}) carries the full residual. That is the RNN channel the user excluded." That finding is why PURE-ANNEAL became §211's main cell. SYNTH2 item 6 reverses it.
- **V0-OFF** is lifetime-limited memory, and the **age floor μ ≥ 2** is fading memory. The frozen draft lists both under "Not adopted … Destructive, fading or lifetime-limited memory: the user's constraint" (THEORY_VERSIONBANK_DRAFT §4).

- **The evidence does not buy the cost either.** The citation behind the floor (CoTFormer 2609.19521, via LIT2) reports recent-2 through recent-8 "near chance" and only recent-1 at 99.4%.
  - A floor of 2 nats leaves the age-1 version at e^−2 ≈ 13.5% relative weight. That is the recent-2 regime the paper says fails.
  - So the floor pays the constraint cost without the cited benefit. Only W = 1 has support, and W = 1 is the most RNN-like option.
- **The floor also closes nothing where the race is decided.** S's "race" is decided in stage 1 (n ≤ 3, t ≤ 3).
  - There V̂_1 is the newest version or at age 1, at a cost of ≤ 2 nats.
  - V̂_1 is the whole input minus one reduction.
  - "V0-OFF: no path from V_0 except via V̂_1 [PROVED]" (D §4) is true but vacuous for recompute in that regime.

**Fix.** Put the question to the user as such, with the constraint-faithful variant VB-2G (§C). Before any spend, run the $0 eval-time arms on the §211 cells: V0-OFF, age floor and W = 1 imposed at eval only (descriptive, beside HIST-TRUNC). If they don't raise DEPTH on a trained cell, there is no evidence to trade the constraint for.

### 4. MAJOR: COMMUTE makes every value error absorbing, and current models do recover
**The measurement** [MEASURED, `recover.py`, 187-3 rows]: after a wrong teacher-forced handle at t−1, the handle at t is right 0.34–0.49 of the time in all nine models checked.

| class | P(right \| wrong at t−1) | P(right \| right at t−1) |
|---|---|---|
| failed | 0.40–0.44 | 0.79–0.83 |
| converged | 0.35–0.49 | 0.90–0.96 |

The aux is a reader, so this mixes reader noise with real state repair. Either way, errors are not absorbing in today's trajectories.

**What COMMUTE does.**
- It removes repair by construction. The handle target follows the decoded (wrong) operands.
- Carried literal slots copy dec V_{t−1}, so a corrupted literal is trained to stay corrupted ("owning mistakes").
- R1-C trains the coda to report the wrong value.
- DEEP at n = 8 then needs per-step accuracy ≥ 0.987 (S itself, G-ABSORB). 11 of 12 converged INJECT models have q ≥ 0.949 in the worst class.

**Pre-register (Stage 0, $0).** VB REPAIR on the §211 rows:
- P(value right at t + 1 | value wrong at t, skeleton right);
- P(final correct | ≥ 1 step wrong).

**Rule.** If REPAIR ≥ 0.2 in a DEEP §211 cell, use S's "bank-repair" variant instead of COMMUTE. In that variant, carried slots keep true targets whenever an older version decodes them, and COMMUTE applies only at the handle's value slot.

  - With value weight 2, L_X starts near 2·ln 11 ≈ 4.8 nats on edited items: comparable to the whole loss, before the model can reduce at all.
  - On an N2 base, 211-12's candidate mechanism (c) (a diffuse gather mixing edited and unedited entries) applies to OPX directly.
  - **Fix:** if OPX runs at all, ramp it in after curriculum stage 1.
- **CANON** (B2). Its starting gap is at chance level [MEASURED, `canon_gap.py`, 64 items, pretrained GPT-2, CARRY pass 1]:

  | comparison | per-slot RMS-normalised MSE | cos |

  | block-9 output V_1 vs Φ_1 (fresh prelude of the remainder) | 0.494 | 0.753 |

  - So L_can asks four pretrained blocks to become a layer-6 → layer-6 near-identity from a near-chance start.

### 6. MAJOR: after STALL-AUG, the stall-family clock tests can be passed by a counter STALL-AUG cannot penalise
**The counters.**
- Trained stalls are exact duplicates. Two counters are blind to them:
  - a distinct-version count;
  - **literal copy-age drift** in the null space. A copied version adds no drift, so the literal slots' drift counts c_t exactly, at no cost.
- Both pass STALL-SHIFT and double or triple stalls.
- Both keep "c_t ≥ 9 ⇒ anything" unconstrained, because c_t ≤ 8 on every training item.
- H's Prop 8 constrains the **bank index** only. This is §190's "done + remaining > 8" channel, left open.

**Decisive held-out clock tests:**
- **(i) PF on natural items** (untrained by stalls; it catches both counters).
  - Its CLOCK-KEYED label reads *history-length*-keyed: a larger k also means more history. HIST-TRUNC beside PF separates clock from history.
- **(ii) A new FOREIGN-INSERT / FOREIGN-REPLACE pair.**
  - Insert o ∈ {3, 6} versions of a length-matched unrelated item at the old end of the bank (the index shifts by o; no duplicate signature), against replacing o existing old entries by foreign ones (same disruption, index unchanged).
  - The difference is the clock effect. It is comparator-free and untrained.
- **STALL-SHIFT, triple stall and REWIND become descriptive.**
  - REWIND is confounded: it is the only version that ever *grows* in length, which a content-keyed model may fail on (FRAGILE).
- **C5-VB:** replace STALL-SHIFT with PF ∧ FOREIGN, and keep DEPTH-TIMING. DEPTH-TIMING at n 9–10 still discriminates the c_t counter.

### 8. MAJOR: physical compaction silently breaks fixed-T code paths; implement it as masks
Each of these would mis-index without crashing [VERIFIED]:
- `bias_row` expands one `pad` over every version (vbank.py:127). Variable m_s needs per-version masks.
- The coda drops V_0's columns by slicing `[..., T_:]`, assuming each version is T_ wide (vbank.py:206–207). With shorter versions it drops V̂_1's leading slots instead.
- `answer_logits` reads `lens − 1` (vbank.py:257–259). After compaction that index is a pad or the wrong slot, so the answer is read off " =".
- `nrm` uses per-original-slot rms0 (vbank.py:220, 238).
- The gather's `wpe[:T]` queries and its `s_idx / i_idx` layout assume a T-grid (vbank.py:179–183).

**Fix:** compaction as key masks plus re-indexed stamps on the fixed T grid.
- **Equivalence [DERIVED].** GPT-2's attention sees position only through stamps, so a masked VOID key is exactly a dropped key; queries at dropped slots are discarded.
- **Cost.** It is shape-stable with no per-item heterogeneity. Because the step is launch-bound (211-3), physical compaction would save nothing and add gather/scatter launches.
- **The restamp must be applied at storage** (to the stored V̂_t before bank K/V and gather keys), not only to the next pass input (D M2). Otherwise:
  - every bank entry and the coda's causal V̂_t keep stale stamps (a residual size cue);
  - a PA gather's position query wpe[j] matches the slot that *was* at j, a misalignment of δ ∈ {2, 4} right of the handle.

**Other traps.**
- `bias_row` is module-level, so ssvb.py cannot override it as a method.
  - Re-implement `rollout` / `coda` in the subclass. Never monkeypatch `VB.bias_row`: a §211 eval in the same interpreter would silently change.
  - Gate: run one §211 checkpoint through vbank_eval after `import boxes.ssvb` and require identity in item counts.
- **Decorators.** `mct` (vbank.py:264) and `calibrate` (:284) are `@torch.no_grad`; so are the eval parts part_acc :100, part_vswap :262, part_stall :310 and part_tf :332.
  - `vswap_class` (:268) is undecorated but only ever runs under :262's no_grad.
  - The OPX training branch will be written next to 211-15's OPERAND-FOLLOW eval and is the likely copy source. Reusing it zeroes L_X's gradient.
  - nested170's `aux_greedy` :274 and `dec_reads` :581 stay off-limits (REDTEAM F9d).
  - Per-term path gradflow (L_X, L_can, R1-C, the kept-slot gradient through the masked bank) is required, with decorators checked by ast.
- **The prelude stamps absolute wpe** (deltamem.py:266), and GPT-2 position also lives in derived features (the NoPE argument: causal attention recovers position).
  - So M2 fixes at most the direct component, and inexactly: cancellation needs a = g·ρ/rms(V) per slot and pass.
  - **POS-PROBE** (decode o(j) at matched compacted j) must be a registered descriptive.
  - A positive POS-PROBE is the expected case without (self-)CANON.

## C. A constraint-faithful variant (VB-2G)

**Design.** The memory stays attention-only and fully readable. Recency and history-blindness are *learned* under training pressure, not imposed.
1. **Input.** The N2 gather. Under compaction (masks), the queries are wpe[compacted j] and the keys are restamped at storage. That removes C-gq's absolute-size content, plus D's relative ω on the newest version.
2. **Age bias.** Learned ALiBi as in §211, with no floor; V_0 stays readable.
3. **HISTORY-SWAP** (replaces V0-OFF, the age floor and STALL-AUG).
   - With probability p_h = 0.25 per item, at passes t ≥ 2, every bank entry older than the newest (V_0 included) is replaced by the stored entries of another item in the same micro-batch.
   - The foreign history has a uniform length in {0..K_max}.
   - **No extra forward:** the entries are already computed. Masks handle the lengths.

**Claims.**
- **H1 [PROVED, as Prop 8(ii)].** On swapped items the bank index is independent of the newest content's progress. So "t ≥ 9 ⇒ hold" and the count rule pay L_rec > 0 on a positive-probability set.
  - The (index, remaining) support then covers index + remaining up to K_max + 8. That is more than STALL-AUG's band, and with no duplicate signature to detect.
- **H2 [DERIVED].** On swapped items, I(target; old entries | newest) = 0. So any read of history is zero-mean noise there, and the recompute reward of free-running targets falls by the factor (1 − p_h).
  - **Not proved:** a swap detector (consistency of literals across versions) could restore history use on unswapped items.
