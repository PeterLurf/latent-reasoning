# Pre-registration check: F6 (age-transplant training), F7 (path doubling on pair labels), §54b estimators (2026-09-30)

Literature session, for Theory; Audit copied. IDs verified (Semantic Scholar). "(full text)" = read from the paper.

## F6 — content-matched aged-state transplant as training augmentation
- **Nearest:**
  - **Interchange Intervention Training** (IIT, Geiger et al. 2112.00826): during training, swap the hidden
    representation of a causal variable from a source input into a base input, and supervise the counterfactual output.
    F6 is IIT with the variable = frontier node and the swap = an *aged* latent from another graph. The closest
    precedent.
  - **Anil et al.** 2211.09961 (full text): path independence is induced by *content-agnostic* means: hidden states
    initialised "with zeros on half of the examples … standard Gaussian noise on the rest", and "randomized compute
    budgets/depths".
  - Bansal 2202.05826 progressive loss: continue from the *same input's* own earlier state.
  - Growing NCA sample-pool training; Huginn 2502.05171 random s₀; scheduled sampling 1506.03099 and professor forcing
    1610.09038 (feeding the model's own states or outputs); Manifold Mixup 1806.05236 (hidden-state mixing across inputs).
- **New if scoped as:** transplanting a *content-matched, aged* latent across inputs specifically to decouple age from
  content against count-keyed stops, with answer-only supervision. Not found. **Medium–low:** cite IIT as the method
  family and Anil as the path-independence rationale.
- **Risks:**
  - The recipient may read a residual age signal from the donor latent.
  - Report a matched control: a same-age, content-*mismatched* transplant, and an age-only reset (cf. the external
    hypothesis test).

## F7 — log-depth path doubling over readable pair labels
- **Nearest:**
  - **Edge Transformers** 2112.00578 (triangular attention over node pairs, i.e. composition through a k-intermediate).
  - Relational Attention 2210.05062.
  - Triplet-GMPNN, *A Generalist Neural Algorithmic Learner* 2209.11142 (triplet edge reasoning for Floyd–Warshall-type
    algorithms).
  - Pair representations with triangle updates (AlphaFold-style).
  - Mechanistic: Saparov 2412.04703 finds transformers learn *exponential path-merging*, with reachable sets doubling per
    layer.
  - Theory: log depth suffices (Merrill & Sabharwal 2503.03961; Sanford 2402.09268); pointer jumping and
    repeated-squaring transitive closure are classical.
- **New if scoped as:** a *readable* Boolean pair state iterated with doubling in a latent-reasoning loop, trained
  label-free, with C7 edit tests on pair labels. Not found in latent reasoning or LMs. **Medium:** the components are
  known (Edge Transformer, triplet NAR, path-merging).
- **Risks:**
  - O(n²) pair tokens.
  - Saparov reports learning gets harder with graph size; Ye 2510.19753 finds capacity limits beyond 3^L.
  - Soft ∨/max may blur and needs hardening (see §54b).

## §54b — estimator IDs and the "soft-trained, hardened-at-test" gap
- **IDs confirmed:**
  - Straight-through / stochastic neurons: Bengio, Léonard, Courville 1308.3432 ✔
  - Gumbel-softmax: Jang et al. 1611.01144 ✔
  - Concrete: Maddison et al. 1611.00712 ✔
  - Also: VQ-VAE 1711.00937 (straight-through codes); Binarized Neural Networks 1602.02830 (STE training).
- **The hardening gap:**
  - **Soft-to-Hard Vector Quantization** 1704.00648 anneals soft → hard to close the train/test mismatch.
  - The **DARTS discretization gap** (1806.09055; Zela et al. 1909.09656, "Understanding and Robustifying DARTS") is the
    best-known case where a continuous relaxation degrades when hardened. Zela's fixes are flatter minima and early
    stopping.
  - **Soft Tokens, Hard Truths** 2509.19170: for continuous CoT trained with RL, "the best-performing scenario is to
    train with continuous CoT tokens then use discrete tokens for inference". This is a direct LM precedent that the gap
    can be favourable.
- **Suggestion:** report the hardened-vs-soft accuracy gap per run, and anneal temperature (Soft-to-Hard VQ) rather than
  hardening abruptly.
