# Prior check for §100: GEN-M at k = 2 on mult 4×4 / 5×5 (2026-10-03)

Literature session, for Theory; Audit copied. Brief. arXiv IDs as verified earlier (LIT_INDEX).

## 1. Prior on the latent/text interleave ratio
- **Coconut 2412.06769:** c continuous thoughts replace each text step (GSM8K uses c = 2). Its ablation over c on
  GSM8K reports gains as c grows (latents per step, not snap frequency).
- **CoLaR 2505.16552:** a compression factor merges c tokens per latent. Accuracy falls as compression rises, and the
  factor can be chosen at inference.
- SuperThoughts 2606.13862 (2 CoT tokens per latent); Token Assorted 2502.03275 (codes replace the first m steps);
  SWITCH / ThinkRouter (routed interleave).
- 2510.14095 snaps every iteration (≈ k = 1). Our own: §83 (fixed k = 4 exact; k = 16 fails out of range), §97 (k = 1
  is the written-state loop).
- **No published snap-frequency sweep for multiplication or arithmetic.** §98 (k = 4) vs §100 (k = 2) would be the first.

## 2. Pitfalls specific to k = 2
1. **Bypass is very plausible at k = 2.**
   - The fork at slot 2 sees the start checkpoint (S_{j−1}), a, b_j and b_{j+1} in context. S_{j+1} = S_{j−1} +
     a·b_j·10^j + a·b_{j+1}·10^{j+1}: two partial products and an add, in one pass.
   - ICoT-SI shows GPT-2 small can internalise an *entire* 4×4/5×5 product in one pass (0.998/0.996). So two steps in
     one pass is well within reach, and the slot-1 latent can be ignored.
   - **MASK2 is essential, not optional.** Also run slot-1 removal (ablate the slot-1 latent with the ops visible) on
     GEN2. If GEN2 holds with slot 1 ablated, the k = 2 result is "text checkpoints every 2 ops", not latent carriage.
2. **The compute claim is only vs text CoT.**
   - At inference GEN2 writes the step text every 2 ops (about half of TC-M's step tokens) plus latent passes.
   - The released ICoT-SI writes no CoT and is already at about 1.0. At matched accuracy it dominates on FLOPs, so
     scope "cheaper" to "vs text CoT".
   - Meter sequential passes as well as FLOPs.
3. **The odd final chunk at 5×5 (op 5 alone)** has no hand-off. Make sure length-1 final chunks appear in training (if
   training also chunks by 2 over 5 ops, they do). Report per-chunk accuracy, so a final-chunk failure isn't confused
   with hand-off failure.
4. **The range is fine here.** At 4×4/5×5 the checkpoint text ("+ P_j ( S_j )", j written zeros) stays within its
   trained length. The range issue in LIT_S98_MULT applies only to longer multipliers.

**Reading if GEN2 passes:** with MASK2 and slot-1 removal both showing latent use, it is "one latent hand-off between
text checkpoints suffices on 4×4/5×5". Otherwise it is a text-checkpoint result.
